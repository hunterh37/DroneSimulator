🚁 Drone Courier: Dual Play Concept
🎯 Two Core Modes
Delivery Mode (Zone-Based Gameplay)
Players define Cargo Rooms, Delivery Rooms, and Doorway Zones around their home.
Missions: Deliver crates between zones under time pressure.
Challenge: Multi-zone routes, timed requests, doorway events.
Tone: Arcade, structured, replayable “game mode.”
Sandbox Mode (Chill Couch Play)
No timers, no zones.
Packages of different sizes spawn in front of you.
Use claw gesture to grab, move, drop, or even stack crates however you want.
Packages can have fun physics (wobble, bounce, break open with confetti).
Tone: Relaxed, playful, “toybox” vibe.
🔄 How They Integrate
Unified Controls: The claw grab + flight system is identical in both.
Shared Assets: Cargo boxes, critter props, hubs, delivery pads — reused between modes.
Progression Link:
Sandbox time counts as practice (you can test claw handling, package weight).
Unlock new crate types, skins, or silly physics toys in Sandbox after completing Delivery missions.
Session Flow:
From main menu, choose Play Delivery (structured) or Play Sandbox (relax).
📦 Sandbox Features
Spawn Packages: Tap menu → spawn Small, Medium, Large crates.
Manipulate: Grab with claw, stack, toss, drop.
Mini-Toys: Unlockables like glowing cubes, bouncing spheres, or critter dolls that can be picked up.
No Fail State: Just fun physics interactions in your living room.
🕹️ Delivery Features (Zone-Based)
Defined Rooms: Cargo → Doorway → Delivery.
Timed Missions: Deliver X crates in Y time.
Critter Clients: Pop up in delivery zones requesting specific crate sizes.
Doorway Events: Slowdown scanners, critters crossing, random time penalties.
