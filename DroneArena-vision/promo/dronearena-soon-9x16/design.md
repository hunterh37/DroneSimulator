# Drone Arena: Neon Hangar

Concept: your living room is a synthwave hangar. The icon's three layers (grid horizon, orange/cyan
speed ring, courier drone) are the grammar of every scene: a cyan grid floor, glowing rings that read
as race gates and delivery gates, and the low-poly drone that flies through each one.

Palette
- void #0D0820 (backgrounds, 3D clear color, from the icon sky)
- signal #38E6F5 (grid, gate rims, HUD strokes)
- heat #FF8A1F (speed ring, accents, second gate color)
- paper #F2ECDD (display type)
- dim #6B6488 (secondary mono labels)

Type
- Display: Archivo Black 400, uppercase, tracking -0.04em, paper color.
- Telemetry: JetBrains Mono 700, uppercase, tracking 0.2em, signal or dim.

Motion
- Cuts on the 107.7 bpm beat (0.557s). Hard entries (expo.out 0.35s).
- Every 3D scene ends on a gate or on the drone and hands off by flash or circle reveal.
